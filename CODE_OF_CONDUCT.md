# Code of Conduct

Feynman Studio is a small, open scientific project. We want it to be a welcoming place for everyone who uses it or contributes to it, whatever their background, career stage, country or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a language that is not their own.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Disagree respectfully and explain your reasons.
- Give credit: cite the work of others and acknowledge their contributions.
- Accept constructive criticism, and give it constructively.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory or sexualised language or images.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Any other behaviour that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic setting.

## Scope and reporting

This code applies to the issues, pull requests and discussions of the repository and to any other space where someone represents the project.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, write to the maintainer at alessandromoreci27@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues that do not follow this code, and may block anyone who repeatedly breaks it.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
